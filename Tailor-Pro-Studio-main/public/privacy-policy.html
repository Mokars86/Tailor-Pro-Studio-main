Privacy Policy

App Name: Tailor Pro Studio (TailorPro) | Package: com.tailorpro.studio | Effective Date: August 23, 2026

Tailor Pro Studio respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our atelier management tools.

1. Information We Collect

Tailor Pro Studio collects data to provide seamless tailoring studio management, client measurements, and inventory tracking:

  • Client Profiles & Measurement Data: Names, phone numbers, garment specifications, and measurement values (e.g., chest, waist, hips, sleeve length) entered or dictated by you.
  • Camera & Photo Access: When you use our AI Fabric Color Scanner or Face & Back Weave Inspector, the app accesses your device camera or selected photos strictly to analyze fabric patterns and color spools.
  • Payment Transaction Data: Subscription upgrades and graduation fee payments are processed securely via Paystack. We do not collect or store credit card numbers, Mobile Money PINs, or banking credentials.
  • Local Storage Data: App settings, client databases, and inventory records are stored locally on your device for offline operation.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To generate client measurement charts, order invoices, and official apprentice graduation certificates.
  • To provide AI-powered fabric matching and voice dictation measurement processing.
  • To manage your active subscription tier and verify payment transactions via Paystack.
  • To improve app performance, stability, and customer support.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or client data to third parties. We interact with trusted third-party services exclusively for app functionality:

  • Paystack Gateway: Handles secure Mobile Money (MTN, Telecel, AT) and Card payment processing. (Paystack Privacy Policy)
  • Google Gemini AI API: Processes fabric image analysis and voice dictation text conversion.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data security is paramount. Client records and measurements remain on your device using encrypted local storage. Any network communications with our servers or payment partners are encrypted using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data within Tailor Pro Studio:

  • Access & Modify: You can edit or update client profiles and measurement records at any time.
  • Delete Data: You can delete individual client files or clear application data directly from the app or your device settings.
  • Data Removal Requests: To request complete account or transaction record removal, contact our support team at WhatsApp +233546920418.

6. Children's Privacy

Tailor Pro Studio does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Our application is designed for professional tailors, atelier studios, and fashion designers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published directly on this page with an updated effective date.
